QuakeSignal is an independent desktop app for monitoring selected earthquake information feeds.

See recent reports, review local event history, choose the data sources you want to follow, and set a minimum magnitude and optional distance filter. Configure a standard tone, urgent tone, or original Japanese safety voice, along with volume, drill handling, and routine-report notifications.

The Japanese voice is independently synthesized safety guidance, not an official J-Alert or JMA recording. Its HTS Voice Mei source is credited under CC BY 3.0 inside the release materials.

The macOS edition stores its history and preferences on your computer. It has no account system or cloud synchronization. It connects directly to the Wolfx Open API, which aggregates selected Japan Meteorological Agency (JMA) and Chinese national and regional earthquake-network feeds.

While QuakeSignal is running, it evaluates incoming feed reports locally and can present a local foreground alarm for reports that match your settings. The Mac app does not use App Attest or Apple Push Notification service and is not a background emergency-alert service.

QuakeSignal is not affiliated with JMA, CENC, or any government agency. Information from upstream sources can be delayed, incomplete, revised, or inaccurate. Always follow official local emergency guidance during an earthquake.
