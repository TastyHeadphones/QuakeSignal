QuakeSignal is an independent monitoring app. It does not require an account,
sign-in, payment, or a backend service operated by the developer.

The app opens on the Home tab and connects directly to selected public
earthquake-information feeds through the Wolfx Open API. Live information can
change, be delayed, or be revised. macOS may ask for notification permission on
launch; notifications are optional, and a reviewer can decline that request and
still inspect the app.

The Mac App Store build does not expose a Test Alarm control. Location is
entered manually in Settings; the app does not request macOS Location Services
access.

For review, use Settings to select a language, source feeds, magnitude
threshold, optional location/radius, and local notification preferences. The
Mac App Store build intentionally does not expose Launch at Login.

Settings also offers a standard tone, urgent tone, and an original synthesized
Japanese safety voice. The Japanese option is not a J-Alert or JMA recording;
HTS Voice Mei by Nagoya Institute of Technology is attributed under CC BY 3.0
in the included release evidence.
The app does not display an estimated seconds-until-shaking countdown.

The Mac app evaluates feed updates locally while it is running and may present
a local foreground alarm matching the selected settings. It does not use App
Attest or APNs and is not a background emergency-alert service. Notification
delivery and sounds remain subject to macOS and user settings.
