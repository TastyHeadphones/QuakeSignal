APPLE WATCH COMPANION
Open QuakeSignal on Apple Watch to refresh a compact headline and recent earthquake reports, then view available magnitude, status, source, time, intensity, and depth details. While the Watch app is open, a fresh active JMA earthquake early warning can present protective-action guidance with the native Watch warning haptic. The QuakeSignal Urgent Tone or Japanese Safety Voice selected on the paired iPhone is mirrored to Watch and may also play while the app is active. The companion does not independently register for push notifications, App Attest, Time Sensitive or Critical Alerts, or background emergency delivery; optional background alerts remain on the paired iPhone.
