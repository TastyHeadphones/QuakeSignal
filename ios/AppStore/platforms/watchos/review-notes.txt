The Apple Watch app is a foreground-only companion embedded in the QuakeSignal
iOS 1.1 (8) upload. It has no account, sign-in, payment, location permission,
notification permission, demo account, or reviewer credential.

Open the Watch app to refresh a compact headline and recent-report list, then
open a report for available magnitude, status, source, time, intensity, and
depth details. It fetches only while opened and does not independently register
for APNs, App Attest, Time Sensitive or Critical notifications, alert audio, or
background delivery. Optional nearby alerts remain an iPhone feature; do not
review the Watch companion as an independent emergency-alert service.

The Watch app must be tested from the signed iOS host build on a paired Apple
Watch before submission. Simulator/generic compilation is not evidence for
pairing, installation, Digital Crown/scroll behavior, or foreground refresh.

QuakeSignal is independent and non-official. Wolfx-aggregated information can
be delayed, incomplete, revised, or inaccurate. Always follow official local
emergency guidance.
