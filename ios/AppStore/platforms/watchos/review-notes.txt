The Apple Watch app is a foreground-only companion embedded in the QuakeSignal
iOS 1.2 (21) upload. It has no account, sign-in, payment, location permission,
notification permission, demo account, or reviewer credential.

Open the Watch app to refresh a compact headline and recent-report list, then
open a report for available magnitude, status, source, time, intensity, and
depth details. While the scene is active, a fresh structurally valid early
warning can present a full-screen protective-action view and one native Watch
warning haptic per event serial. The iPhone's selected System Default, original
QuakeSignal Urgent Tone, or licensed Japanese Safety Voice preference is mirrored
with WatchConnectivity. System Default uses only the native warning haptic;
either custom choice also plays its bundled audio while the Watch scene remains
active. Screenshot automation uses finalized historical reports and never
starts feedback.

The Watch app fetches only while opened and does not independently register for
APNs, App Attest, Time Sensitive or Critical notifications, or background
delivery. Optional background alerts remain an iPhone feature; do not review the
Watch companion as an independent background emergency-alert service.

Build 21 reads Wolfx feed identifiers jma_eew, jma_eqlist, cenc_eew,
cenc_eqlist, sc_eew, fj_eew, and cq_eew, plus USGS, EMSC, and GeoNet catalog
companions. QuakeSignal formats and normalizes that factual information; it does
not calculate a new local-intensity or ground-motion-arrival forecast.

The Watch app must be tested from the signed iOS host build on a paired Apple
Watch before submission. Simulator/generic compilation is not evidence for
pairing, installation, preference mirroring, speaker/haptic behavior,
Digital Crown/scroll behavior, or foreground refresh.

Source: Japan Meteorological Agency (JMA) and China earthquake feeds relayed
through Wolfx, plus USGS, EMSC, and GeoNet catalogs, normalized/edited by
QuakeSignal. QuakeSignal is independent and non-official; the information can
be delayed, incomplete, revised, or inaccurate. Always follow official local
emergency guidance.
