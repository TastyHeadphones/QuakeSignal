QuakeSignal for Apple TV is a foreground-only earthquake-information dashboard.
It requires no account, sign-in, payment, location permission, notification
permission, demo account, or reviewer credential.

Open the app to load recent reports. Use the Siri Remote to focus the Refresh
button, move through recent reports, open an event detail, and open Alert Sound.
The Alert Sound screen offers System Default, QuakeSignal Urgent Tone, and
Japanese Safety Voice. System Default is visual-only on tvOS. Either custom
sound starts only after the reviewer presses Preview or Play with the Siri
Remote; the app never starts audio automatically. Japanese Safety Voice uses
HTS Voice “Mei” by Nagoya Institute of Technology under CC BY 3.0 and is not a
J-Alert or JMA recording. The upstream Wolfx JMA feeds may temporarily return
no reports or partial availability; this is an expected network state rather
than a credential gate.

Build 13 reads only Wolfx feed identifiers jma_eew and jma_eqlist. CENC,
Sichuan, Fujian, and Chongqing feeds are disabled. QuakeSignal formats and
normalizes JMA-issued factual information for display; it does not calculate a
new local-intensity or ground-motion-arrival forecast.

This tvOS target intentionally has no APNs, App Attest, Time Sensitive or
Critical Alerts entitlement, automatic alert audio, or background delivery.
While the scene is active it uses validated foreground WebSocket updates with
conservative HTTPS recovery. After the session establishes its current-state
baseline, a new or monotonic updated fresh JMA warning can present a full-screen
visual safety view. Retained/backfilled warnings do not impersonate a new live
alert. Monitoring and user-started playback stop when the scene becomes
inactive. It must not be reviewed as a background emergency-alert service.

The three App Store screenshot frames deliberately remain benign dashboard,
report-list, and finalized-detail views. They do not demonstrate automatic
audio or a fabricated live emergency. The Alert Sound screen, focus containment,
Remote-only playback, and scene-deactivation stop behavior require signed-build
review on Apple TV hardware before submission.

Source: Japan Meteorological Agency (JMA), relayed through Wolfx and
normalized/edited by QuakeSignal. QuakeSignal is independent and non-official;
the information can be delayed, incomplete, revised, or inaccurate. The UI
directs customers to follow official local emergency guidance.
