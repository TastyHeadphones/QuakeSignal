QuakeSignal for Apple TV is a foreground-only earthquake-information dashboard.
It requires no account, sign-in, payment, location permission, notification
permission, demo account, or reviewer credential.

Open the app to load recent reports. Use the Siri Remote to focus the Refresh
button, move through recent reports, and open an event detail. The upstream
Wolfx feeds may temporarily return no reports or partial source availability;
this is an expected network state rather than a credential gate.

This tvOS target intentionally has no APNs, App Attest, Time Sensitive or
Critical Alerts entitlement, alert audio, or background delivery. It refreshes
only while the dashboard is active and must not be reviewed as a background
emergency-alert service.

QuakeSignal is independent and non-official. Wolfx-aggregated information can
be delayed, incomplete, revised, or inaccurate. The UI directs customers to
follow official local emergency guidance.
