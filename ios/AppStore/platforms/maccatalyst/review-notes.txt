QuakeSignal for Mac is the Swift-native Mac Catalyst build from the shared
QuakeSignal target, Apple ID 6800642443, and bundle ID
com.quakesignal.app. It is not the separate Tauri product and it is not the
iPhone/iPad binary offered through Designed for iPad on Mac.

No account, sign-in, payment, demo account, notification permission, or review
credential is required. For a permission-free route, skip location access,
choose a listed city, and review Home, Reports, Map, Guide, and Settings. The
Mac app uses a native resizable window and local app sandbox.

Build 21 (version 1.2) reads Wolfx feed identifiers jma_eew, jma_eqlist,
cenc_eew, cenc_eqlist, sc_eew, fj_eew, and cq_eew, plus USGS, EMSC, and GeoNet
catalog companions. QuakeSignal formats and normalizes that factual information;
magnitude and distance controls filter presentation only and do not calculate
predicted local intensity or ground-motion arrival time.

This Mac Catalyst route is foreground/local-only. It does not independently
register for APNs or App Attest and does not provide background emergency-alert
delivery. While the app is open, a fresh qualifying warning can take over
the window and use the selected System, Urgent, or Japanese Voice presentation
sound. Closing or backgrounding the app stops monitoring and alert audio; this
is not an Apple Critical Alert and does not claim to bypass system sound or
Focus controls.

Source: Japan Meteorological Agency (JMA) and China earthquake feeds relayed
through Wolfx, plus USGS, EMSC, and GeoNet catalogs, normalized/edited by
QuakeSignal. QuakeSignal is independent and non-official; the information can
be delayed, incomplete, revised, or inaccurate. Always follow official local
emergency guidance.
