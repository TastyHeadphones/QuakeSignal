QuakeSignal for Apple Vision Pro brings earthquake reports, nearby context, and practical preparedness guidance into a native window.

FOLLOW EARTHQUAKE INFORMATION
Review normalized JMA earthquake early warnings and reports relayed through the Wolfx Open API. Preliminary, updated, final, cancelled, and training messages use distinct status treatments.

UNDERSTAND WHAT IS NEARBY
Choose a city or use your current location to review distance, direction, magnitude, available intensity, depth, timing, and epicenter context on the map.

FOREGROUND SAFETY CONTEXT
Apple does not list Push Notifications or Time Sensitive Notifications as supported visionOS provisioning capabilities. QuakeSignal for Apple Vision Pro therefore does not register for APNs or App Attest and does not claim background emergency-alert delivery. While the app is open, it refreshes earthquake information and can present a qualifying live warning in the app with your selected local alert sound. Close or background the app and that foreground monitoring stops.

PREPARE OFFLINE
Keep drop-cover-hold-on guidance, situation-specific safety actions, an emergency-kit checklist, and family check-in notes available without a network connection.

QuakeSignal is independent and is not affiliated with JMA or any government agency. JMA-issued information relayed through Wolfx can be delayed, incomplete, revised, or inaccurate. In-app warnings are best-effort and are not a government emergency-alert service. Always follow official announcements and local emergency instructions.
