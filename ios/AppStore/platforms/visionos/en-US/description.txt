QuakeSignal for Apple Vision Pro brings earthquake reports, nearby context, and practical preparedness guidance into a native window.

FOLLOW EARTHQUAKE INFORMATION
Review normalized JMA and China earthquake early warnings and reports relayed through the Wolfx Open API, plus USGS, EMSC, and GeoNet catalog companions. Preliminary, updated, final, cancelled, and training messages use distinct status treatments.

UNDERSTAND WHAT IS NEARBY
Choose a city or use your current location to review distance, direction, magnitude, available intensity, depth, timing, and epicenter context on the map.

FOREGROUND SAFETY CONTEXT
Apple lists App Attest for visionOS, but does not list Push Notifications or Time Sensitive Notifications as supported visionOS provisioning capabilities. QuakeSignal's protected alert registration requires both App Attest and APNs. Because APNs is unavailable on this target, QuakeSignal for Apple Vision Pro does not start that registration path or claim background emergency-alert delivery. While the app is open, it refreshes earthquake information and can present a qualifying live warning in the app with your selected local alert sound. Close or background the app and that foreground monitoring stops.

PREPARE OFFLINE
Keep drop-cover-hold-on guidance, situation-specific safety actions, an emergency-kit checklist, and family check-in notes available without a network connection.

QuakeSignal is independent and is not affiliated with JMA, CENC, USGS, EMSC, GeoNet, or any government agency. Information relayed through Wolfx and companion catalogs can be delayed, incomplete, revised, or inaccurate. In-app warnings are best-effort and are not a government emergency-alert service. Always follow official announcements and local emergency instructions.
