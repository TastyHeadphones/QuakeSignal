QuakeSignal for Apple Vision Pro is the full native, windowed experience. It
requires no account, sign-in, payment, demo account, or reviewer credential.

For a permission-free route, skip location access during onboarding and choose
a listed city. Home, Reports, Map, Guide, and Settings remain reviewable.
Location is optional. This target does not request notification permission.

Apple's current visionOS provisioning-capability table lists App Attest, but
does not include Push Notifications or Time Sensitive Notifications.
QuakeSignal's protected alert registration requires both App Attest and APNs;
because APNs is unavailable on this target, the Release app does not start that
registration path. It obtains no APNs token, does not invoke App Attest for
alert registration, and has no Time Sensitive, Critical Alerts, or background
emergency-alert path. While the app is open, a qualifying update can
be presented in the app and play the selected local alert sound. Closing or
backgrounding the app stops that foreground monitoring. Simulator screenshots
do not prove this runtime behavior.

Build 21 (version 1.2) reads Wolfx feed identifiers jma_eew, jma_eqlist,
cenc_eew, cenc_eqlist, sc_eew, fj_eew, and cq_eew, plus USGS, EMSC, and GeoNet
catalog companions. QuakeSignal may format, localize, filter, and normalize
those facts, but does not calculate a new local-intensity or ground-motion-arrival
forecast and does not issue an official government warning.

The alert-sound picker includes system sound, an original urgent tone, and an
original synthesized Japanese safety voice. The voice is not a J-Alert or JMA
recording; HTS Voice "Mei" by Nagoya Institute of Technology is credited under
CC BY 3.0 in the release evidence.

App Motion is a required visionOS portal answer. Do not select it from these
notes alone. The previously recorded source assessment for this windowed
interface is that the app does not contain high motion and has no intentional
virtual-camera movement.

Source: Japan Meteorological Agency (JMA) and China earthquake feeds relayed
through Wolfx, plus USGS, EMSC, and GeoNet catalogs, normalized/edited by
QuakeSignal. QuakeSignal is independent and non-official; the information can
be delayed, incomplete, revised, or inaccurate. Always follow official local
emergency guidance.
