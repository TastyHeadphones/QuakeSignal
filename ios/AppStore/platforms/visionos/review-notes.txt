QuakeSignal for Apple Vision Pro is the full native, windowed experience. It
requires no account, sign-in, payment, demo account, or reviewer credential.

For a permission-free route, skip notification and location access during
onboarding and choose a listed city. Home, Reports, Map, Guide, and Settings
remain reviewable. Location and notifications are optional.

When a customer opts into nearby notifications, the Release target uses App
Attest before APNs registration. Qualifying fresh warnings may be marked Time
Sensitive, subject to system and user settings. This target has no Critical
Alerts entitlement and does not claim guaranteed delivery or Silent/Focus
bypass. Physical/TestFlight notification behavior is a release gate, not
something inferred from a simulator or generic build.

The alert-sound picker includes system sound, an original urgent tone, and an
original synthesized Japanese safety voice. The voice is not a J-Alert or JMA
recording; HTS Voice "Mei" by Nagoya Institute of Technology is credited under
CC BY 3.0 in the release evidence.

App Motion is a required visionOS portal answer. Do not select it from these
notes alone. The release owner must assess the final Apple Vision Pro build;
source inspection shows a windowed interface and no intentional virtual-camera
movement.
