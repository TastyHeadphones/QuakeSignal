QuakeSignal for Apple Vision Pro is the full native, windowed experience. It
requires no account, sign-in, payment, demo account, or reviewer credential.

For a permission-free route, skip location access during onboarding and choose
a listed city. Home, Reports, Map, Guide, and Settings remain reviewable.
Location is optional. This target does not request notification permission.

Apple's current visionOS provisioning-capability table does not include Push
Notifications or Time Sensitive Notifications. The Release target therefore
has no APNs, App Attest, Time Sensitive, Critical Alerts, or background
emergency-alert path. While the app is open, a qualifying JMA-issued update can
be presented in the app and play the selected local alert sound. Closing or
backgrounding the app stops that foreground monitoring. Simulator screenshots
do not prove this runtime behavior.

Build 8 reads only Wolfx feed identifiers jma_eew and jma_eqlist. CENC,
Sichuan, Fujian, and Chongqing feeds are disabled. QuakeSignal may format,
localize, filter, and normalize JMA-issued facts, but does not calculate a new
local-intensity or ground-motion-arrival forecast and does not issue an
official government warning.

The alert-sound picker includes system sound, an original urgent tone, and an
original synthesized Japanese safety voice. The voice is not a J-Alert or JMA
recording; HTS Voice "Mei" by Nagoya Institute of Technology is credited under
CC BY 3.0 in the release evidence.

App Motion is a required visionOS portal answer. Do not select it from these
notes alone. The release owner must assess the final Apple Vision Pro build;
source inspection shows a windowed interface and no intentional virtual-camera
movement.

Source: Japan Meteorological Agency (JMA), relayed through Wolfx and
normalized/edited by QuakeSignal. QuakeSignal is independent and non-official;
the information can be delayed, incomplete, revised, or inaccurate. Always
follow official local emergency guidance.
