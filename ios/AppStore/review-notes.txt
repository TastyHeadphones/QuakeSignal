QuakeSignal is an independent, non-official earthquake-information app. Build 14
reads only the Wolfx jma_eew and jma_eqlist feeds; CENC, Sichuan, Fujian,
and Chongqing feeds are disabled. It formats JMA-issued factual information and
tells people to follow official local emergency guidance. Data can be delayed,
incomplete, revised, or inaccurate; notifications are best-effort and are not a
government emergency-alert service.

No account, sign-in, payment, demo account, or review credential is required.
For a permission-free review path, start the app, choose Skip at the
notification and location onboarding steps, then use the Home, List, Map, Guide,
and Settings tabs. Alternatively, a reviewer can choose a listed city instead of
granting location access.

Notifications are optional. If enabled, the app uses a selected city or rounded
location plus the chosen JMA feed type, magnitude, and radius filters to route
JMA-issued reports. Those settings do not calculate predicted local intensity
or ground-motion arrival time. Qualifying fresh warnings may be marked Time
Sensitive, subject to the user's notification and Focus settings. This release
has no Critical Alerts entitlement, does not bypass the mute switch as a Critical
Alert, and does not guarantee delivery. The public Release includes the ordinary, foreground
Send Test Alert control only after notification authorization, an opt-in alert
registration, and an APNs token are present. The InternalQA-only delayed
background-training control is not compiled into a public Release archive, and is
not needed for App Review.

Settings offers System Default, QuakeSignal Urgent Tone, and Japanese Safety
Voice. The Japanese option is an original safety sentence synthesized with HTS
Voice “Mei”; its voice model is attributed under CC BY 3.0 in the in-app sound
detail and checked-in audio provenance. It is not copied from or presented as
J-Alert, JMA, government, carrier, or broadcaster audio. Foreground playback
and APNs presentation remain subject to the person's sound, volume, and Focus
settings; this release does not claim Critical Alert behavior.

The embedded Apple Watch app is a foreground-only companion. Open it to refresh
a compact headline and recent reports, then open an event for available details.
While open, a fresh active JMA warning can show protective-action guidance with
the native Watch warning haptic. The selected QuakeSignal custom sound is mirrored
from the paired iPhone and may also play while the Watch scene is active. It does
not independently register for APNs, App Attest, Time Sensitive or Critical
notifications, or background delivery. Optional background alerts remain an
iPhone feature. Review the companion from the signed iOS host on a paired Apple
Watch; no separate Watch IPA or reviewer account is required.

Source: Japan Meteorological Agency (JMA), relayed through Wolfx and
normalized/edited by QuakeSignal. QuakeSignal is independent and is not
affiliated with or endorsed by JMA.

Settings links to the Privacy Policy and Support pages. The optional Terms URL in
the release worksheet is not currently an in-app Settings link and must not be
described as one. The production service uses the public HTTPS endpoint
https://quakesignal-api.hopeso.workers.dev; no private CA or client certificate is
required.
