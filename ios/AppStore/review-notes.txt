QuakeSignal is an independent, non-official earthquake-information app. It reads
public Wolfx Open API feeds and tells people to follow official local emergency
guidance. Data can be delayed, incomplete, revised, or inaccurate; notifications
are best-effort and are not a government emergency-alert service.

No account, sign-in, payment, demo account, or review credential is required.
For a permission-free review path, start the app, choose **Skip** at the
notification and location onboarding steps, then use the Home, List, Map, Guide,
and Settings tabs. Alternatively, a reviewer can choose a listed city instead of
granting location access.

Notifications are optional. If enabled, the app uses a selected city or rounded
location and chosen thresholds to decide whether a nearby earthquake report may
warrant a local alert. The public Release includes the ordinary, foreground
**Send Test Alert** control only after notification authorization, an opt-in alert
registration, and an APNs token are present. The InternalQA-only delayed
background-training control is not compiled into a public Release archive, and is
not needed for App Review.

Settings links to the Privacy Policy and Support pages. The optional Terms URL in
the release worksheet is not currently an in-app Settings link and must not be
described as one. The production service uses the public HTTPS endpoint
https://quakesignal-api.hopeso.workers.dev; no private CA or client certificate is
required.
