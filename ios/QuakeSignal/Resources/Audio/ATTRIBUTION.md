# QuakeSignal alert-audio provenance

Neither file in this directory copies, samples, or impersonates a J-Alert,
Japan Meteorological Agency, government, carrier, or broadcaster recording.

## `quakesignal_urgent.caf`

The two-note waveform and its generator are original QuakeSignal work,
copyright 2026 UniSphereco LLC and distributed under the repository's MIT
license. It is generated by `ios/AppStore/scripts/generate-alert-audio.py`.

## `quakesignal_japanese_voice.caf`

QuakeSignal wrote and synthesized this original safety message:

> 地震情報です。強い揺れに備え、身の安全を確保してください。

English meaning: “Earthquake information. Prepare for strong shaking and
secure your safety.” The wording is deliberately generic and does not claim to
be an official warning.

Generation uses `pyopenjtalk==0.4.1` at speed `1.08`, with its bundled HTS
Voice “Mei,” followed by mono 22.05-kHz signed 16-bit PCM CAF conversion using
FFmpeg. The reviewed pyopenjtalk source revision was
`0f0fc44e782a8134cd9a51d80b57b48a7c95bb80`.
